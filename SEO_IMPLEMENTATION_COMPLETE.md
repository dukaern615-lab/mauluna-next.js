## 📋 Completed SEO Enhancements:

### 1. ✅ Dynamic Sitemap (`app/sitemap.ts`)
**What it does:** Auto-generates a list of all pages for Google to crawl
**Result:** Google finds and indexes all your properties automatically
**Location:** Accessible at `https://mauluna.it/sitemap.xml`

### 2. ✅ Optimized Robots.txt (`public/robots.txt`)
**What it does:** Tells search engines which pages to crawl and which to skip
**Changes:**
- Blocks private pages (login, dashboard, admin)
- Allows property pages and images
- Points to sitemap
- Added crawl-delay for server performance

### 3. ✅ Breadcrumbs with Schema (`components/Breadcrumbs.tsx`)
**What it does:** Shows navigation path + tells Google your site structure
**Example:** Home > Immobili > Parioli > Property Name
**Result:** Better SEO ranking + Rich snippets in Google
**Location:** Added to all property pages

### 4. ✅ FAQ Schema (`components/FAQSchema.tsx`)
**What it does:** Structured data for Frequently Asked Questions
**Result:** Your FAQs can appear directly in Google search results
**Location:** Added to homepage with 5 common questions

### 5. ✅ Enhanced Local Business Schema (`app/layout.tsx`)
**What it does:** Tells Google you're a local business in Rome
**Includes:**
- Business name, address, phone
- Opening hours
- Service area (Rome)
- Ratings
- Social media links
**Result:** Better visibility in "near me" searches + Google Maps

### 6. ✅ Enhanced Property Schema (`components/feature/PropertyMetaTags.tsx`)
**What it does:** Rich product data for each property
**Includes:**
- Property type, price, size
- Location details
- Availability status
- Seller information
**Result:** Rich snippets with property details in Google search

### 7. ✅ All Domain URLs Updated
**Changed:** `mauluna-immobiliare.com` → `mauluna.it` everywhere
**Files updated:**
- app/layout.tsx
- components/feature/PropertyMetaTags.tsx
- netlify.toml (domain redirects)
